Calisthenics

A side passion — strength & movement with bodyweight

Why calisthenics?

Besides coding, I spend a lot of time on calisthenics. It’s a way to build strength, mobility, and discipline using only bodyweight — no gym required. Pull-ups, push-ups, dips, and progressions like front lever and planche keep training interesting and goal-oriented.

I also built a small Workout Planner in Python that uses the Google Gemini API to generate weekly plans — a mix of my interest in software and fitness.

If you’re into calisthenics or want to talk training, feel free to get in touch.
